# Classification and width evidence

[Official activity-space improvement plan](https://www.119.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=F1447C02C95C3A04), revision 2015-08-28, provides contextual red/yellow administrative scope criteria. Red uses widths 2.5–4m with dead-end length over 30m or connected length over 60m; yellow uses over 4–5.5m with dead-end length over 60m or connected length over 120m. The plan separately discusses kept-clear width/height. These are not probabilities, casualty indices, current obstruction or an instruction to reconstruct a category from width alone. The accepted CSV's official categories are preserved exactly: 196 紅區, 77 黃區.

The [current alley page](https://www.119.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=C1778E86611A3CEE) links a PDF titled 1150730-臺北市狹小巷道(紅、黃區)清冊.xlsx, whose header explicitly labels 寬度(公尺). This establishes metre context for that attachment. It does not establish the CSV's survey date, minimum/nominal/measured semantics, complete edition identity or present-day vehicle clearance. A full row-by-row PDF-to-CSV unit crosswalk was not established; PDF visual extraction was unavailable during source research. Unit feasibility is contextual, not admitted numeric semantics for the accepted CSV.

All 273 accepted B widthRaw cells are numeric-looking source strings; no missing or range cells occur in this edition. Decimal/range/missing/unknown-unit fixtures exercise the parser. The helper's numericLooking output is a format diagnostic only. widthNumeric stays null and widthUnit stays unknown. Numeric-width distributions and width-dependent scenario controls are disabled; raw values can be inspected in source context. quality.sources.B.rawWidthByCategory counts exact **strings**, not certified numerical metres.

The explorer's converter `scripts/convertNarrowAlleyRegistry.ts` deliberately requires an explicit 公尺 or m suffix inside each cell. Its 274 retained numeric-only cells therefore have widthMeters=null. This contract and its raw/generated files are unchanged. No arbitrary passability threshold exists.

[Official fire-access Q&A](https://www.119.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=226AD0C4D0209EB5) gives contextual recognition categories: connected brick/wood groups; dense buildings without water sources; large class-A basements; high rises; specified care/hospital institutions; underground MRT; enclosed mechanical parking towers; large department-store/retail facilities; and other formally recognized difficult-rescue places. These nine descriptions are contextual and do not constitute an edition-bound CSV dictionary. Unknown or blank numeric codes are flagged; 1–9 are retained without inferred severity. The grade 1/2 calculation and threshold contract was not found. C grade counts: 1=81, 2=330.

A original 列管要件 categories: 火災搶救困難地區 116; 囊底路 20; 社區參與 23; 長巷道 25; combined 火災搶救困難地區及囊底路 1. The [management procedure](https://www.119.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=7D3E570C7532A588) provides administrative context. Its boundary wording and the Q&A differ around 7.5m; this system does not implement a new threshold classifier. Full exact definitions for the accepted edition remain a documentary question.

Missing dictionary and width-method evidence are follow-up issues, not fire-risk weights. Source rating is never a building structural condition, fire likelihood, truck clearance, arrival-time or fatality prediction.
