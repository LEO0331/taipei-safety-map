# Geographic evidence contract

**No Policy #12 geometry is admitted.** All 869 documentary rows have zero admitted coordinates/segments; the policy UI deliberately has no map, pins, inferred lines, geocoding, proximity analysis, routing or travel-time simulation. Existing explorer maps are unrelated and remain available.

A/B/C provide names, administrative codes and descriptions without an authoritative shared road-segment ID, compatible segment geometry or verified exact place extent. A road-name string is not a physical segment. C can refer to a building group, intersection, area, facility or address range; even an apparent building number does not provide validated official coordinates.

Official GIS exists: [臺北市區界圖](https://data.gov.tw/dataset/121199), Taipei dataset 1601ef3a-c253-4988-b047-943d9e786143, resource d8b7eb29-136f-49fc-b14b-3489d3656122, describes TWD97 and a resource labelled 20220915. A district polygon cannot position an unlocated alley or establish compatible segment identity. Its reference edition is distinct from these lists, so it is not fetched into the client or used to certify published district membership. The audit preserves the official catalogue page as contextual evidence.

A historical [official road-centerline programme document](https://www-ws.gov.taipei/001/Upload/public/Attachment/21261137416.pdf) discusses distinct data versions and TWD67/TWD97 migration. It is not an admitted current road-segment crosswalk. No compatible A/B/C identity/extent crosswalk was established in this bounded investigation; this is not a claim that the city has no road GIS.

Future admission requires authoritative segment identifiers and geometry, CRS/axis validation, edition/reference compatibility, proven record-to-segment extent crosswalk, clear reuse terms and adversarial tests. Geometry alone still cannot establish actual operational access. Required operational evidence additionally includes current surveyed widths/height, turning radii, road closures/construction/parked obstructions, vehicle dimensions, staffing/deployment availability, dispatch events/timestamps and official validation.

Missing operational values stay unknown. No current road is labelled passable/impassable, safe/unsafe or a preferred rescue/evacuation route.
